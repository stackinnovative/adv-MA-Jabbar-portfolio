About
A

Abdul Jabbarudeen M.

Advocate · Mediator & Arbitrator · Postgraduate in Law & ADR, NALSAR

Adv. Abdul Jabbarudeen M. has over two decades of experience in law, arbitration and alternative dispute resolution (ADR).

He holds a postgraduate degree in Law and ADR from NALSAR University of Law, Hyderabad. His work focuses on commercial and infrastructure-related disputes.

He is a certified mediator with the Indian Institute of Corporate Affairs (IICA) and the International Mediation Institute (IMI), and an empanelled arbitrator with the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and BSE.

His approach to mediation rests on empathy, dialogue and understanding — using them to help parties move from conflict to cooperation.

How Mediation Works

From dispute to settlement

  1. STEP 01

    First meeting

    Understanding the dispute and checking whether it is suited to mediation or arbitration.

  2. STEP 02

    Joint & private sessions

    Each side is heard, together and separately, in confidence.

  3. STEP 03

    Finding common ground

    Options are explored openly until terms both parties can accept emerge.

  4. STEP 04

    Settlement agreement

    The agreed terms are written down and signed, so they can be enforced.
